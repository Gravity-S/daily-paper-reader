## 摘要
基于LLM的多智能体系统（MAS）在复杂问题求解中展现出潜力。随着MAS方法日益多样化，系统化评估变得愈发具有挑战性。然而，现有基准大多关注最终结果，导致协作增益如何产生、如何保持或如何丧失仍不清楚。为弥补这一局限，我们提出MASTraceBench，一个通过MAS中的提案轨迹来诊断协作增益的基准。在六个合作与竞争任务中，MASTraceBench跟踪并评分提案轨迹，并提供多层指标套件，涵盖任务得分、协作增益、提案轨迹指标和Token成本。利用MASTraceBench，我们不仅根据最终性能，还根据智能体提案如何演化并聚合为最终答案，对代表性MAS方法进行系统比较。该分析揭示了一个反复出现的模式：MAS的最终答案很少超越最强的初始提案；交互往往将初始较弱的提案提升至接近该最强提案，而强初始提案很少被进一步改进，且可能退化。为降低这一风险，我们提出CLEARS，它用跨智能体的主张级评估替代整体提案交换，以引导可靠的综合。CLEARS更常保留或改进最强初始提案，并在六个任务中的五个上取得最高的协作增益。

## Abstract
LLM-based multi-agent systems (MAS) have shown promise in complex problem solving. As MAS methods diversify, systematic evaluation becomes increasingly challenging. However, existing benchmarks largely focus on final outcomes, leaving unclear how collaboration gains arise, are preserved, or are lost. To address this limitation, we introduce MASTraceBench, a benchmark for diagnosing collaboration gains through proposal trajectories in MAS. Across six cooperative and competitive tasks, MASTraceBench tracks and grades proposal trajectories and provides a multi-layer metric suite covering Task Score, Collaboration Gain, proposal-trajectory indicators, and Token Cost. Using MASTraceBench, we systematically compare representative MAS methods not only by final performance, but also by how agent proposals evolve and are aggregated into the final answer. This analysis reveals a recurring pattern: final MAS answers rarely surpass the strongest initial proposal; interaction often lifts initially weaker proposals toward it, while strong initial proposals are seldom further improved and may regress. To reduce this risk, we propose CLEARS, which replaces whole-proposal exchange with claim-level evaluation across agents to guide reliable synthesis. CLEARS more often preserves or improves upon the strongest initial proposal and achieves the highest Collaboration Gain on five of the six tasks.
